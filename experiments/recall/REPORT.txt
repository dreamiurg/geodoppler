recall: why 30 of 34 answered held-out livestreams are wrong, and what recovers them

VERDICT
Mostly the method, not a bug. Of the 30 wrong answers plus the 2 non-answers:
  A  3 looped audio (the stream replays one ~41-47 min recording; the go-live label cannot be right)
  B  5 a search event's fitted speed (124-172 m/s) has no non-jet ADS-B track at the truth time
  C  2 non-jet tracks exist for both search events, but no pair is time-locked
  D  9 a truth-time pair with signal (null P <= 0.07) or a better-verifying point exists but is outranked
  E 12 a truth-time pair exists, but its rank is no better than chance (one exists for most fake times)
  F  1 crash (exit 70, out of memory), fixed
Two bugs were found and fixed: verification clipped tracks to a box and dropped fast or sparse passes (D:
Kz69coKyH9Q, TbVt7uWmnlQ), and offset_filter's join ran out of memory (F: LYoUoV2jRL8, d2EqZDmIw_k decoy 5).
With both fixes, held-out (heldout.py run + decoys, variant `whole`, same 36 cases, decoy rule unchanged):
  correct 4 -> 6, wrong 30 -> 29, none 2 -> 1; wrong-high 0 -> 0 (decoy rule), 1 -> 1 (margin rule, 7NzTIUwkPE8).
  Cost: Cs4nYfpcC0E (correct, low, 0.7 % margin) turns wrong, and UMf6oAlorlk's decoy ratio drops 1.21 -> 1.19,
  so decoy-rule correct-high goes 3 -> 2. The 3 cases gained are all low. Recall is still 6 of 36: 22 of the 30
  failures (A, B, C, E: 3 + 5 + 2 + 12) have no evidence the right aircraft is findable at all.

GROUND TRUTH AND RULES
Sound only. CORRECT = found start within 60 s of the YouTube go-live (release_timestamp), as heldout.py.
The search bbox is labeled.json's search_bbox, set from the channel's stated region (experiments/dataset);
I did not use it to judge spots and did not check whether it contains the true spot (unknown for 32 cases).
The time-given runs use the same bbox.

METHOD (all scripts in experiments/recall; data in /Volumes/DevScratch/pinpoint/exp/recall)
1. rank.py / null_rank.py: in main's candidates/verified.parquet, the best combination whose start is within
   60 s of go-live, and its rank. Null: the same lookup at 40 fake truths (go-live +-10..200 min). Most cases
   have a "truth-time" candidate by chance (18-29 of 40 fake truths hit), so rank alone proves nothing;
   nullP = share of fake truths that rank at least as well.
2. given.py: `locate --start <go-live> --start-window 60 --decoys 5` on all 36 (decoys at the same time of
   day). given_table.py, given_vs_main.py compare it with main.
3. loopscan.py / loop.py: spectrogram autocorrelation over lags 60 s-1 h.
4. gates.py: for the no-match cases, the search front half at the truth time with gates relaxed one by one
   (rotorcraft, speed, slant, offset, same aircraft for both events). Jets cannot be tested: props.parquet
   never held them.
5. extras.py: at the time-given answer, every aircraft near each unexplained extra event, vs the verify gates.
6. reverify.py: verification only, re-run on main's candidates (search unchanged): points per combination
   1 vs 8, box-clipped vs whole tracks. points=1 reproduces main's verified scores exactly.
7. joinsize.py: rows of the unblocked offset join on LYoUoV2jRL8's crashing tile.

EXIT 70
lockf exits 70 (EX_SOFTWARE) when its command is killed by a signal. LYoUoV2jRL8, Orlando tile: 24.7M x
19.6M (point, pass) candidates; the unblocked offset_filter merge would build 949,401,850 rows (~84 GB of
numbers) on a 36 GB machine; killed right after "event 2" is logged. d2EqZDmIw_k decoy 5 died at the same
step (21.0M x 33.1M). Fix (commit 0e31d13): join in blocks of 50k grid points, filter each block, restore the
original row order; bit-identical output (test + a randomized comparison with the old function). The blocked
join gives 407,258 hits on that tile.

FIX KEPT
1. offset_filter in blocks (0e31d13): no behavior change, removes the OOM. LYoUoV2jRL8 now answers, correct
   (err -4 s, 2.707, margin 13 %, k 1.00/1.15 -> low; decoy ratio 1.18). d2EqZDmIw_k's decoy 5 now completes.
   LYoU's 853 s is the slowest locate (one tile: 347k hits, 10.8k combinations).
2. verify_extra on whole tracks (d673cdd): the aircraft that enter an extra event's box are verified on their
   full +-400 s track instead of the box-clipped part (gates unchanged). Re-verifying main's candidates alone
   (reverify_1w.json): Kz69coKyH9Q and TbVt7uWmnlQ become correct, Cs4nYfpcC0E wrong.
   Limit: the segment's global CPA within +-400 s is scored, not the one nearest the locked time, so a
   loitering aircraft can now lose a match the clipped pass had (likely why UMf6oAlorlk's own score fell
   3.798 -> 3.752). Not refined: picking the nearest CPA would be one more choice tuned on this set.
